## QR Code Format

```
dcwlt://pay?u=USER_ID&k=TIME_KEY&s=SIGNATURE
```

Parameters:
- `u`: Customer's Privy ID (URL encoded)
- `k`: TOTP time-based key (60-second windows)
- `s`: HMAC-SHA256 signature

## Payment Flow

1. Merchant adds items to cart (via merchant inventory)
2. Cart stored in `localStorage` as `merchantCart`
3. Merchant taps "Scan Customer QR" button
4. Scanner page validates cart (redirects if empty)
5. Camera opens with scan overlay
6. Customer QR is scanned and parsed
7. Payment mutation called with customer ID, time key, signature, merchant ID, amount
8. On success: Vibration, cart cleared, redirect to dashboard
9. On error: Show error message, option to retry
